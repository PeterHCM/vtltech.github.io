Classification into equipment groups and categories (Directive 2014/34/EU)

Equipment Group I – mines endangered by firedamp: categories M1 and M2

M1
For use in underground parts of mines and surface installations endangered by firedamp and/or combustible dust. The equipment shall remain functional even in the event of rare incidents with an explosive atmosphere present: if one means of protection fails, an independent second means provides the requisite level of protection, or protection is assured with two independent faults.
M2
For use in underground parts of mines and surface installations endangered by firedamp and/or combustible dust. If an explosive atmosphere occurs, it must be possible to switch off the equipment. Constructional measures ensure safety in normal operation, even under severe conditions, rough handling and changing environmental influences.

Equipment Group II – all other hazardous areas: categories 1, 2 and 3

1
For areas where an explosive atmosphere is present continuously, for long periods or frequently. Even with infrequent equipment failures it must remain safe: if one protective measure fails, an independent one ensures safety, or safety is ensured with two independent faults in combination.
2
For areas where an explosive atmosphere occurs occasionally. Even with frequent equipment failures or normally expected faults, constructional measures ensure the required safety.
3
For areas where no explosive atmosphere from gases, vapours, mists or whirled-up dust is expected; if it occurs, only rarely and briefly. Safety is ensured in normal operation.

Example of type label: CEAG product

  1. Name or registered trade mark (CEAG) and address of the manufacturer
  2. Serial number including year of manufacture
  3. Certificate number, may end with “X” (special conditions for safe use apply) or “U” (component certificate)
  4. Additional IECEx certification
  5. Marking according to directive: equipment group (II), category (2), atmosphere G (gas, vapour or mist) – D (dust)
  6. Marking according to standard IEC/EN
  7. Equipment name/type
  8. CE marking and number of the notified body monitoring the quality system (0158 = EXAM Germany)
  9. Electrical parameters
  10. Other essential information (depends on the standard, e.g. lamp)
  11. Other optional information (e.g. degree of protection)
  12. Permissible ambient temperature (−25 °C to +55 °C); no marking required for −20 °C to +40 °C (standard values)
  13. Marking according to EU directive 2002/96/EC (WEEE)
