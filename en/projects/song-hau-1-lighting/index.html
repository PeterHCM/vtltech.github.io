Scope of supply

From 2018 to 2020 VTL supplied Eaton Crouse-Hinds (USA) industrial and hazardous-area lighting for the Song Hau 1 thermal power plant:

  • nVMV 150 W metal halide luminaires, ATEX Ex nR, Class 1 Zone 2, IP66
  • ARCH 150 W HPS street lights, 240 VAC 50 Hz
  • Outdoor LED floodlights IP67, 5000 K, 20,000 lm, 100–277 VAC
  • Tufflite 2×36 W outdoor fluorescent fittings, IP66, GRP body
