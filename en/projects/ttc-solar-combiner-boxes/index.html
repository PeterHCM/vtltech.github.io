Scope of supply

In 2018 VTL supplied DC combiner boxes manufactured by Weidmüller Spain for TTC Energy solar projects:

  • Krong Pa solar farm – 49 MW (Gia Lai)
  • Tay Ninh 01 solar farm – 68 MW
  • Tay Ninh 02 solar farm – 50 MW

The boxes integrate string fuses, DC surge protection and isolators, configured to each plant's string layout.
