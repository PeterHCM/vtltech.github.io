Scope

In 2021, under the “Supply of Top-Up Control Parts” package, VTL supplied Eaton MTL intrinsically safe fieldbus barriers.

Eaton MTL 9372-FB redundant fieldbus barrier

  • For FOUNDATION™ fieldbus networks in hazardous areas
  • Redundant configuration for very high system availability
  • Complete enclosure systems for up to 6 IS spur connections
  • Failure alarm direct to the host via integrated fieldbus device
  • Mounts in Zone 1 (gas) or 21 (dust) with spurs into Zone 0
