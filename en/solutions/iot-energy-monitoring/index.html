IoT & energy monitoring

Real-time monitoring of energy, air quality and flow on IoT platforms.

Overview

Real-time operating data helps optimise energy, detect faults early and plan maintenance. VTL deploys sensors, gateways and monitoring platforms sized to each plant.

What VTL provides

  • Multi-point energy monitoring with threshold alarms
  • Indoor air quality (IAQ) monitoring
  • Smart IoT-connected vortex flow meters
  • Weidmüller u-control M3000/M4000 EDGE controllers
  • Dashboards, reports and SCADA/cloud connectivity

Contact VTL engineers for a site survey and a tailored proposal.
