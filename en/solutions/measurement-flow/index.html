Measurement & flow

Pressure, temperature and custody-transfer fuel flow measurement with genuine instruments.

Overview

Accurate measurement underpins safe operation and custody transfer. VTL supplies genuine instruments and supports selection, calibration and replacement.

What VTL provides

  • Baumer pressure and temperature gauges and sensors
  • Liquid Controls positive-displacement (PD) meters for fuels
  • Faure Herman turbine and ultrasonic flow meters
  • Eaton tank-truck earthing equipment
  • Emerson AMS Trex and MTL HART configuration tools

Contact VTL engineers for a site survey and a tailored proposal.
