Surge protection & earthing

Surge protection for signals, data networks and power; earthing and tank lightning protection.

Overview

Lightning-induced surges are a leading cause of instrument and control failures at plants and gas stations. VTL has upgraded surge protection for PV GAS, Long Son, Nghi Son, Hai Linh LNG and more.

What VTL provides

  • MTL SD, TP48 and IOP signal protectors for 4–20 mA, HART and fieldbus loops
  • MTL SLP, ZoneDefender PRO and Weidmüller VARITECTOR power protection
  • MLDT surge protector testers
  • Lytech RBC retractable bypass conductors for floating-roof tanks
  • Survey and upgrade of lightning protection at gas stations and fuel depots

Contact VTL engineers for a site survey and a tailored proposal.
