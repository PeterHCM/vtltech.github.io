Power distribution & quality

MV/LV switchgear, protection, motor control, UPS, power-quality monitoring and surge protection with Eaton Power Distribution and Eaton MTL.

Overview

With Eaton Power Distribution and Eaton MTL, VTL designs, supplies and installs power systems from MV substations to LV switchboards, motor control and uninterruptible power – with power-quality monitoring and surge protection for plants, buildings and data centres.

Medium-voltage distribution

  • Power Xpert UX MV switchgear for primary distribution up to 24 kV
  • SF6-free Xiria ring main units for secondary distribution
  • MV vacuum circuit breakers and EDR 3000/5000 protection relays
  • Bussmann MV fuses for transformers and motors

Low-voltage distribution and protection

  • xEnergy Main switchboards to IEC 61439 for MSBs, DBs and MCCs
  • IZMX ACBs, NZM MCCBs, xEffect FAZ MCBs – see Power distribution products
  • NH fuses and 170M semiconductor fuses

Motor control and automation

  • DILM contactors, PKZM motor protection, DS7 soft starters, PowerXL DG1/DM1 drives
  • SmartWire-DT, XV300 HMI, easyE4 programmable relays
  • RMQ-Titan M22 pushbuttons and pilot lights, LS-Titan position switches

Uninterruptible power for critical loads

  • 5E, 5P, 9SX, 9E single-phase UPS, 9PX and the 93PM three-phase UPS
  • ePDU rack PDUs and Bussmann supercapacitors for short-term backup

Power quality and monitoring

  • Power Xpert Meters: energy, harmonics, sags and swells
  • Capacitors and MV-AutoVAR automatic banks for power factor correction
  • Connection to VTL's IoT & energy monitoring platform

Surge protection and power filtering (Eaton MTL)

  • MTL SLP and ZoneDefender PRO power surge protection for distribution boards – see Surge protection products
  • MA15/MA15E mains filters and surge protectors for PLC, DCS and remote I/O cabinets
  • LS range LED lighting surge protectors

VTL services

  • Site surveys, power-quality and energy audits
  • Design, supply, installation and commissioning of MV and LV panels
  • Maintenance, thermographic surveys, retrofits and upgrades

Contact VTL engineers for a site survey and a tailored proposal.
