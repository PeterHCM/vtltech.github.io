Solutions

Automation & control

Smart marshalling, signal isolation, remote I/O and DCS/PLC integration for plants and offshore platforms.

Overview

VTL supplies the components between the control system and field devices – where loop reliability is decided – together with design, panel building and integration services.

What VTL provides

  • MTL SUM5 smart marshalling, MTL4500/5500 isolators, MTL7700 Zener barriers
  • MTL4850 HART multiplexers for asset management
  • Weidmüller u-remote distributed I/O and u-control controllers
  • Weidmüller relays, power supplies and signal conditioners
  • DCS/PLC/SCADA integration, control and marshalling cabinets

Contact VTL engineers for a site survey and a tailored proposal.
