About Us

Since 2008, Viet Thang Loi Technology (VTL) has delivered engineering solutions, genuine equipment and EPC services to safety-critical industries.

2008founded
15+years of experience

An engineering partner for Vietnamese industry

Viet Thang Loi Technology Company Limited (VTL) was founded in 2008 to supply solutions, services and equipment for industrial and hazardous-area environments: oil and gas, chemicals, power, pulp and paper, marine and more.

As the representative of Eaton (MTL Instruments, Crouse-Hinds, Power Distribution), Weidmüller and Azbil in Vietnam, VTL combines genuine equipment with engineers who know international standards, delivering safe, reliable and cost-effective solutions for every project.

Beyond equipment supply, VTL delivers EPC, civil and industrial construction, and MEP works – supporting customers from site survey and design to construction, operation and maintenance.
