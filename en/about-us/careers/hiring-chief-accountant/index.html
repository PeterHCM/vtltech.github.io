Hiring: Chief Accountant

Ho Chi Minh City · Full-time · 5+ years of experience · Finance & Accounting

Chief Accountant

  • Work Location: 19A Cong Hoa Street, Bay Hien Ward, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

  • Employment Type: Full-time

  • Experience: Minimum 5 years of accounting experience, including at least 2 years as a Chief Accountant or Lead General Accountant

  • Department: Finance & Accounting

  • Reports To: Managing Director

  • Salary: Negotiable based on qualifications and experience

About VTL TECH

Viet Thang Loi Technology Company Limited (VTL TECH) provides engineering solutions and services for the Industrial, Energy, Automation, Electrical, and Oil & Gas sectors. We work closely with our customers to supply industrial equipment, deliver technical solutions, and execute projects, with a commitment to creating practical, efficient, and sustainable value.

Learn more about VTL TECH at: go.vtltech.com.vn/profile

Job Responsibilities

  • Oversee and manage all financial and accounting activities of the company.

  • Handle labor administration and social insurance procedures.

  • Manage the accounting system, financial records, invoices, and supporting documents.

  • Advise the Board of Management on financial management, accounting controls, and compliance.

  • Further details will be discussed during the interview.

Requirements

  • Female candidate with a College Diploma or Bachelor's Degree in Accounting.

  • Possess a valid Chief Accountant Certificate.

  • At least 2 years of experience in a similar position.

  • Experience in tax finalization and tax compliance.

  • Good English communication skills are an advantage.

  • High level of integrity, professional ethics, and commitment to legal compliance.

  • Ability to work independently.

Benefits

  • Performance-based bonuses in accordance with company policies.

  • 13th-month salary in accordance with internal company regulations.

  • Allowances and additional benefits based on company policies.

  • Full participation in compulsory social, health, and unemployment insurance in accordance with Vietnamese labor laws.

  • Professional working environment with long-term career development opportunities in the industrial engineering sector.
