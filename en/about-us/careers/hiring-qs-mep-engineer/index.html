Hiring: QS MEP Engineer

Ho Chi Minh City · Full-time · 1–2 years of experience · QS MEP Engineer

1. Job Information

  • Position: Quantity Surveyor – Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing (QS MEP Engineer)

  • Number of Vacancies: 01

  • Work Location: 19A Cong Hoa Street, Bay Hien Ward, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

  • Employment Type: Full-time

  • Salary: Negotiable based on qualifications and experience, with an expected range of VND 10,000,000 – 25,000,000 per month

2. Job Description

  • Technical Drawings: Prepare preliminary MEP design drawings and detailed MEP construction drawings.

  • Quantity Takeoff: Measure and calculate quantities for MEP works, including electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and fire protection systems, based on design drawings.

  • Office and Site Work: Review and manage tender documents and subcontractor contracts, or perform quantity takeoffs directly at construction sites as assigned. Prepare official quotations for customers.

  • Bill of Quantities and Cost Estimation: Prepare bills of quantities (BOQ), cost estimates, and quotations, including material and labor pricing.

  • Project Documentation: Prepare as-built documentation, final accounts, and project settlement documents.

3. Candidate Requirements

  • Education: College diploma or bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical & Electrical Engineering, Refrigeration and Air Conditioning, or related disciplines.

  • Experience: Approximately 1–2 years of relevant experience in MEP quantity surveying, with knowledge of construction and MEP technical codes and standards.

  • Technical Skills: Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel; ability to read and interpret technical drawings; and working knowledge of AutoCAD. Experience with Revit MEP is an advantage.

  • Detail-oriented, honest, professional, cooperative, and approachable.

  • Strong organizational and task management skills.

  • Ability to work independently and collaborate effectively within a team.

  • Good English communication skills are an advantage.

4. Benefits and Compensation

  • Competitive salary negotiable based on qualifications, experience, and suitability for the position.

  • Performance-based bonuses in accordance with company policies.

  • 13th-month salary in accordance with internal company regulations.

  • Allowances and additional benefits in accordance with company policies.

  • Social insurance, health insurance, and unemployment insurance in accordance with Vietnamese labor laws.

  • Professional working environment with long-term career development opportunities in the industrial engineering sector.
