Hiring: Sales Admin

Ho Chi Minh City · Full-time · 1–2 years of experience · Commercial / Sales

  • Location: Ho Chi Minh City
  • Employment type: Full-time
  • Experience: 1–2 years
  • Department: Commercial / Sales

About VTL TECH

Viet Thang Loi Technology Company Limited (VTL TECH) provides engineering solutions and services for the Industrial, Energy, Automation, Electrical and Oil & Gas sectors. We support our customers with equipment supply, engineering solutions and project execution, aiming to deliver practical, efficient and sustainable value.

Learn more about VTL TECH: go.vtltech.com.vn/profile

Job description

  • Support administrative and coordination activities for the Sales team.
  • Track RFQs, quotations, purchase orders, contracts and key deadlines.
  • Prepare quotation packages, sales documents and related forms.
  • Update and manage customer, opportunity, quotation and order data in the ERP system.
  • Monitor quotation status and support Sales with customer follow-up.
  • Prepare regular sales reports.
  • Coordinate with Engineering, Projects, Purchasing and Finance to support the sales process.
  • Support the handover from Sales to Projects when needed.

Requirements

  • College or university degree.
  • 1–2 years of experience as a Sales Admin, Sales Coordinator or Sales Support.
  • Good command of Microsoft Office (especially Excel, Word and Outlook).
  • Well organised, with good document management and progress tracking skills.
  • Good communication and teamwork skills.
  • Able to read and understand basic English emails and documents.
  • Experience in Engineering, Industrial, M&E, Automation, Electrical, Oil & Gas or EPC is preferred.
  • Experience with ERP or CRM is an advantage.

We are looking for a careful, proactive and supportive person who enjoys a professional, customer-focused and results-oriented working environment.

Benefits

  • Competitive income based on ability.
  • Training on ERP, products and the company's sales processes.
  • A professional working environment in Industry, Energy and Automation.
  • Opportunities to learn and grow with experienced engineering and sales teams.
