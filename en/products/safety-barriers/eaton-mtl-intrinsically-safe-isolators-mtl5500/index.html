Overview

The MTL5500 range of intrinsic safety isolators meets the needs of application-focused projects, from single instrument loops to fully equipped cabinets, protecting signals entering hazardous areas.

Slim housings suit many space requirements, and single and multi-channel modules let users choose functionality without sacrificing cabinet space. All signal wiring uses removable connectors.

VTL reference projects: Thi Vai LNG terminal, Ninh Binh fertiliser plant.
