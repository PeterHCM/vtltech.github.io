Eaton MTL intrinsically safe fieldbus barriers

Fieldbus barriers providing intrinsically safe spurs from a high-energy trunk for FOUNDATION™ fieldbus H1 devices – mount in Zone 1/21, spurs into Zone 0.

  • High-energy trunk with intrinsically safe spurs for FF H1 devices
  • Mount in Zone 1 (gas) or Zone 21 (dust) with spurs into Zone 0

  • Complete 6/12-spur enclosures or modules/open-frame for your own enclosure

Overview

MTL fieldbus barriers provide intrinsically safe spur connections from a high-energy trunk, enabling reliable connection to certified FOUNDATION™ fieldbus H1 instruments. They are engineered for hazardous areas and come in a variety of configurations and enclosure materials.

Ranges

  • 9373-FB3 – complete enclosure for 12 IS spurs
  • 9377-FB3 – compact fieldbus barrier module, 12 spurs
  • 937x-FB2 – complete enclosure for 6 or 12 spurs
  • 9372-FB – redundant fieldbus barrier enclosure, up to 6 spurs
  • 9387-FB – open-frame redundant barrier, pre-assembled, up to 5 spurs, for a user-selected Ex e enclosure

VTL supplied 9372-FB barriers to Bien Dong POC (2021).
