Overview

The MTL4500 range of backplane-mounted intrinsic safety isolators protects electrical signals entering potentially flammable hazardous areas, helping keep people and plant safe.

Built around planar transformer technology and highly automated manufacture, MTL4500 is designed for system vendors and project applications such as DCS, ESD and F&G: fast module installation, easy integration, low-power circuits and more I/O channels per module.
