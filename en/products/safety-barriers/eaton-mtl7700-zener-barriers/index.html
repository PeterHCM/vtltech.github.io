Eaton MTL7700 DIN-rail zener barriers

MTL7700 DIN-rail zener barriers divert excess energy safely to earth using zener diodes, resistors and fuses – compact, economical, removable terminals, IS earth via the rail.

Overview

Known for quality and reliability, MTL7700 intrinsically safe barriers use zener diodes, resistors and fuses to divert excess energy safely to ground, preventing sparking or overheating of equipment in hazardous areas.

DIN-rail mounting needs no special hardware; removable terminals ease installation and maintenance and provide loop disconnect, and angled wire entry helps wiring in tight enclosures.
