MTL Instruments

Overview

The MTL SD series protects signal and data lines in industrial control, process automation and communication networks. A slim 7 mm design, multi-stage hybrid circuitry and 20 kA surge capability make it suitable for harsh and hazardous areas.

Main ranges

  • SDX (SD07X, SD16X, SD32X, SD55X) – 2- and 3-wire hybrid protection with fuse/disconnect, for 4–20 mA loops, ESD systems, gas and fire detectors
  • SD – standard models with screen earth terminal
  • SDR – high-bandwidth versions for RS232/422/485 and bus systems, classified as simple apparatus
  • SD32T3 – 3-terminal protection for externally powered 3-wire transmitters
  • SDRTD – for 3-wire RTD temperature sensors

Applications

Oil & gas, chemicals, power and renewables, pulp & paper, marine, DCS/PLC systems and industrial communication networks.
