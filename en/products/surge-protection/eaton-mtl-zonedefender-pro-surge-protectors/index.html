Overview

Eaton MTL ZoneDefender PRO is a range of Type 2 AC power surge protectors for industrial, commercial and infrastructure applications. Multi-stage MOV technology and integrated EMI/RFI filtering protect equipment from high-energy surges and high-frequency noise.

Features

  • 80, 100 or 120 kA (8/20 µs) per phase
  • 4 protection modes: L–N, L–G, L–L, N–G
  • Integrated EMI/RFI filter
  • LED, Form C contacts and audible alarm for remote monitoring
  • Thermal fuses and short-circuit disconnect
  • UL 1449 5th Ed., ANSI/IEEE C62.41, IEC 61643-11
  • NEMA 4X / IP66 enclosure, –40 °C to +85 °C, 10-year warranty

Applications

Main distribution boards, ATS and PDB panels; VFDs, UPS and generators; PLC/HMI/SCADA; data centres, telecom, medical, ports, airports and petrochemical plants.
