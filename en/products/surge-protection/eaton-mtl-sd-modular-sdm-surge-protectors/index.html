Overview

MTL SD Modular (SDM) builds on the proven MTL SD range with a pluggable design: all protection components sit in a removable plug, so modules can be replaced without disconnecting field wiring.

Key advantages

  • Pluggable module for fast maintenance and less downtime
  • Ultra-slim 7 mm width for high packing density
  • Make-before-break design – no loop interruption or false DCS alarms during replacement
  • Optional LED diagnostics and fuse/disconnect versions
  • Backward compatible with the traditional SD range
  • Testable with the MTL MLDT data surge tester

Models

Plugs and bases for 7 V, 16 V, 32 V and 55 V (MDX, MFX, MXL, MXX variants), feed-through plugs and HC high-current bases.
