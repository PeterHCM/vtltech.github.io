Safe, efficient and extremly space-saving

Product range

Surge protection for energy systems

In the energy sector, the VARITECTOR portfolio offers lightning and surge protection solutions for voltages from 75 V to 1,500 V for diverse applications in energy supply, renewable energies, water management, and the process industry. The products are tested according the valid product standards EN 61643-11 (AC), EN 50539-11 (PV) and UL 1449 Ed. 4.

 

Your benefits
  • The arresters are tested according to product standard IEC/DIN EN 61643-11 and UL 1449 Ed.4
  • The arresters can be installed in systems according to IEC/DIN 61643-12, IEC 62305-4
  • No back-up fuse needed up to 315 A
  • An integrated locking clip secures the pluggable arrester
  • Visual display now also in yellow as early warning signal

Surge protection for instrumentation and control (I&C)

Weidmüller's broad product range of VARITECTOR SPC for the I&C sector offers products in a 2-piece, pluggable design and VARITECTOR SSC modular terminals for tension clamp or screw connection.

Your benefits
  • The VARITECTOR Series VSPC and VSSC are tested according to product standard IEC/EN 61643-21
  • The VARITECTOR Series VSPC and VSSC can be installed in systems according to IEC/EN 61643-22
  • The VARITECTOR series VSPC and VSSC can be used in applications according to classes C1, C2, C3, and D1
  • A visual and electrical signaling function is available as an option

 

 

Surge protection for data interfaces

Data transmission between controllers, computers, transducers, or actuators must be extremely reliable in order to avoid errors in the transmission of information. To protect this sensitive area, we offer a large number of suitable surge protection solutions.

 

Your benefits
  • Protects your standard signals such as RS 485 and RS 232
  • Ethernet CAT.6 Protection with PoE+ function
