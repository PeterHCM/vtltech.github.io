Overview

SD32 belongs to the Eaton MTL SD range of surge protectors – high packing density, application versatility, proven hybrid circuitry and simple installation. With built-in fuse and loop disconnect.

Each module provides full hybrid protection for 2- and 3-wire loops, and the ultra-slim footprint lets modules double as feed-through terminals.

Applications

  • 4–20 mA loops, 24 V digital signals
  • ESD, F&G and DCS systems
