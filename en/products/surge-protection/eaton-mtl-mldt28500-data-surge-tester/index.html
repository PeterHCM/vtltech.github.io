Overview

The Eaton MTL MLDT28500 Data Surge Tester is a compact, rugged portable instrument for checking signal and data surge protectors. It tests continuity and leakage current and shows a clear Go/No-Go result, making periodic SPD maintenance fast and reliable.

Features

  • Go/No-Go LED indication
  • Selectable test voltages: 7, 16, 32 and 55 V
  • Continuity and leakage tests for all voltage ratings
  • Dedicated socket for MTL SD Modular plugs
  • Rugged carry case, 90–264 V AC supply
