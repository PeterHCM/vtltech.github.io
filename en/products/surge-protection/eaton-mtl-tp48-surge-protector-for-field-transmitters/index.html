Overview

The MTL TP48 is a surge protector designed to protect field-mounted transmitters against surges caused by lightning or cable-induced transients. It screws directly into a spare conduit entry of the transmitter, needs no extra wiring and adds no resistance to the loop.

Key features

  • Simple installation into a spare ½″ NPT, M20 ISO or G½″ BSP entry
  • Self-restoring after each surge for continuous protection
  • Intrinsically safe and flameproof versions certified to ATEX, IECEx, FM and CSA (IIC, T4–T6)
  • Fieldbus compatible (FOUNDATION fieldbus, PROFIBUS PA), 1 MHz bandwidth
  • 2-, 3- and 4-wire versions protecting +ve, –ve, signal and earth
  • 10-year “No Fuss” warranty

Models

TP48-N / -I / -G (2-wire), TP48-3-… (3-wire), TP48-4-… (4-wire); NDI certified versions available for hazardous areas.

Applications

Oil & gas, refining and petrochemicals, power and renewables, chemicals, pulp & paper, marine, data centres and automation systems.
