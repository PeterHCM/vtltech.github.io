Overview

The MTL SLP range protects power feeds to control and instrumentation equipment against lightning-induced surges and transient overvoltages. Combining SLP on the power side with MTL SD (in the panel) and TP48 (at the field device) protects the complete loop – an approach VTL has deployed at many gas stations and petrochemical plants.

Contact VTL to select the right model for your voltage and load current.
