Overview

Eaton supplies reactive-power compensation from LV to MV: individual capacitors, open-air substation banks and MV-AutoVAR automatic banks that switch steps with the load.

Correct compensation raises power factor, cuts line losses, avoids reactive-power penalties and frees transformer capacity.

Applications

  • Plant and industrial-park substations
  • Large motor loads: pumps, fans, compressors
