Overview

Eaton's EDR 3000 and EDR 5000 distribution relays offer complete metering, protection and control in a single compact case, reducing panel space and wiring.

EDR-3000 suits feeder overcurrent protection; EDR-5000 adds voltage and frequency protection, event and waveform recording and communication for SCADA.

Applications

  • MV feeder and transformer panels
  • Power monitoring and substation SCADA
