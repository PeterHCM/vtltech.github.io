Overview

Eaton ePDUs distribute power from the UPS to rack equipment. Managed and Switched models monitor energy, temperature and humidity and control individual outlets remotely to manage capacity and reduce downtime.

Combine them with Eaton UPS and racks for a matched power solution in server rooms.

Applications

  • Data centres and server rooms
  • Network cabinets in plants and control rooms
