Overview

Power Xpert UX is air-insulated medium-voltage switchgear certified to the latest IEC 62271-100 and -200 standards for global use. It covers the complete range of primary distribution applications – incomers, feeders, bus couplers, metering and MV motor starters.

It uses Eaton vacuum circuit breakers and vacuum contactors, combined with protection relays, metering and communication for integration into power monitoring systems.

Applications

  • Plant and industrial-park substations
  • Power, refining, cement and steel plants
  • MV motor starting
