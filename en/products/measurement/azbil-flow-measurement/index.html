Flow measurement

Overview

Azbil covers every major flow principle for liquids, gases and steam. MagneW electromagnetic flowmeters measure conductive liquids with high stability, in 2-wire, sanitary and open-channel versions.

AX22/AX24 vortex meters suit steam, gas and liquids, with AX24 measuring several variables at once. MVF/MVC thermal mass meters measure gas and compressed air without separate pressure and temperature compensation.

Applications

  • Water, wastewater and chemicals
  • Saturated/superheated steam and compressed air
  • Food, beverage and pharmaceuticals
