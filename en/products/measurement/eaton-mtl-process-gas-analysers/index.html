Overview

With over 30 years of gas application experience, Eaton MTL designs analysers for the job rather than modifying existing products – biogas, power stations and the chlor-alkali industry. Zirconia and thermal-conductivity sensors are made in-house and individually characterised.

The GIR6000 is a next-generation biogas analyser with up to six selectable gas modules. K1650/K6050 monitors check hydrogen purity and purge cycles of hydrogen-cooled turbogenerators to avoid explosive mixtures. KK650 measures hydrogen at every stage of chlorine production, including wet chlorine.

Applications

  • Biogas plants and landfill gas
  • Hydrogen-cooled generators in power stations
  • Chlor-alkali, air separation and gas blending
