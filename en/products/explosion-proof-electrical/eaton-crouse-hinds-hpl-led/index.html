Overview

HPL LED fixtures deliver reliable explosion-proof lighting for Zone 1 and 2 gas and Zone 21 and 22 dust areas, in four models from 3,000 to 8,000 lumens with emergency options.

They are maintenance-free with long life, up to 100 lm/W efficacy and pendant, ceiling, wall, yoke and pole mounting.

Applications

  • Chemical and pharmaceutical plants needing T6
  • Gas and compressor stations in Zone 1
