Eaton Crouse-Hinds

Overview

VTL supplies Eaton Crouse-Hinds and CEAG explosion-proof and industrial lighting for hazardous and harsh areas: refineries, chemical plants, offshore platforms, power plants and fuel terminals.

Typical ranges

  • LED highbays and lowbays (Champ VMV/VMVL)
  • LED floodlights (Champ FMV, FMVA)
  • Linear LED fittings (Tufflite, Pauluhn 700)
  • HID fixtures (nVMV, DMV) and compact fittings (F2MV)
  • CEAG Ex LED luminaires (PXLED) and emergency lighting
  • Hazard-Gard fixtures and IP65/IP66 industrial fittings

Reference projects: Rohm & Haas (Dow) chemical plant, Song Hau 1 thermal power plant.
