Overview

nLLK 15 LED uses long-life LED tubes for general illumination indoors or out in IEC Zone 2 and 22 hazardous environments.

Certified for gas and dust Ex areas, its heavy-duty non-metallic construction suits dusty, wet and corrosive conditions.

Applications

  • Zone 2 areas in chemical and gas processing plants
  • Warehouses and workshops with combustible dust (Zone 22)
