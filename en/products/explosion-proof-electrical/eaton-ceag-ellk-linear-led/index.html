Overview

CEAG eLLK LED is an ideal general light source indoors or out in Zone 1, 2, 21 and 22 hazardous environments. It combines the field-proven eLLK housing and EVG09 ballast with the latest LED technology for 110,000 hours of rated life.

Its heavy-duty non-metallic construction suits areas with dust, gas, vapour, smoke, moisture and corrosive conditions.

Applications

  • Offshore platforms and refineries
  • Chemical plants and corrosive environments
  • Corridors and equipment rooms in Zone 1
