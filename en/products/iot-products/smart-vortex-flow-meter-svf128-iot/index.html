Smart vortex flow meter SVF128 (IoT)

SVF128 smart vortex meter measures steam, gas and liquid flow using the Kármán principle, with integrated pressure/temperature and wireless IoT connectivity.

Request a quote

Overview

The SVF128 smart vortex flow meter uses the Kármán vortex principle to measure fluid velocity and calculate volumetric or mass flow. It is suitable for saturated and superheated steam, compressed air, liquids, industrial gases (nitrogen, oxygen) and fuel gases such as natural gas.

  • Wide range, measuring down to 2 m/s
  • Proprietary vibration-resistant sensor
  • Measures volumetric flow, pressure, temperature and mass flow
  • No moving parts – no wear
  • Wireless connectivity: LoRaWAN, Sigfox, NB-IoT, Sub-GHz, 3G/4G
