Overview

The WT actuator is installed on discharge valves of waste transport systems to open and close them automatically by electrical signal, remotely via limit and torque switches, and manually by handle in an emergency.

The IP67 enclosure includes a thermistor-controlled space heater to prevent internal condensation.

Applications

  • Discharge valves in pneumatic waste transport systems
  • Fast-acting 90° valves
