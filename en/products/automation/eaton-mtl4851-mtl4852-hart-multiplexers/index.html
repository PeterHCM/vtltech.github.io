Overview

The MTL4851 (master) and MTL4852 (slave) provide a simple interface between smart field devices, control systems and HART instrument management software on a PC.

They unlock the diagnostic data already inside HART devices for condition monitoring and status-change alerts, complementing the 32-channel SIL3 MTL4850 multiplexer.

Applications

  • Predictive maintenance of field instruments
  • Connecting AMS, PRM or FDM to HART devices
