Eaton MTL 9470 gigabit intrinsically safe Ethernet

Intrinsically safe gigabit Ethernet for Zone 1: gateways, fibre media converter, managed switch, isolator and Wi-Fi – with PoEx™ power and data over one cable.

  • 10/100/1000 Mbps in hazardous areas
  • PoEx™ – intrinsically safe power and data over one Cat 5e/6 cable

Overview

The MTL 9470-ET/G range brings gigabit Ethernet into hazardous areas using Ex ia intrinsic safety. PoEx™ delivers intrinsically safe power and data over a single Cat 5e/Cat 6 cable and allows live connection of end devices in Zone 0 and Zone 1.

Components

  • 9471-ET(G) – serial (RS232/485/422) to Modbus TCP gateway
  • 9475-ETG – single-mode 1310 nm fibre media converter, 10 km
  • 9476-ET(G) – 6-port managed switch
  • 9478-ET/X(G) – isolator mounted in Zone 2 with links into Zone 0
  • 9479-ET(G) – dual-band Wi-Fi access point / bridge

All units share a compact 42 × 160 × 140 mm footprint.
