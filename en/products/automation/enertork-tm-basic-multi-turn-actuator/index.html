Overview

TM Basic is a multi-turn actuator operated only from a separate external control panel. It drives valves, dampers and sluice gates directly, and butterfly or ball valves when combined with a gearbox.

A high-torque, low-inertia motor reaches full torque immediately at start-up, and the built-in thermostat protects the motor. A mechanical switch unit detects torque and opening, stops at the set position and torque, and can be readjusted as needed.

Applications

  • Gate and globe valves in water and wastewater plants
  • Dampers and sluice gates
  • Butterfly and ball valves (with gearbox)
