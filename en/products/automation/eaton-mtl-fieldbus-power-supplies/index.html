Eaton MTL fieldbus power supplies (918x, FISCO 910x/912x, F10x)

Power supplies for FOUNDATION™ fieldbus H1: 918x N+1 redundant, 910x/912x FISCO intrinsically safe and F10x single-segment units.

  • 918x: first N+1 redundant design, up to 25% lower cost per segment
  • 910x/912x FISCO: fully intrinsically safe, live-workable field network
  • F101/F102/F104 single-segment, isolated, compact; F104 low-power for solar sites
  • Host-specific integrated versions and physical-layer diagnostics options

Overview

Eaton MTL covers every fieldbus power requirement. The 918x range is the first to use an N+1 architecture: two modules for segments up to 250 mA and three for 500 mA instead of 1:1 redundancy, reducing capital cost per segment by up to 25%.

FISCO supplies – 910x redundant and 912x simplex – create fully intrinsically safe field networks that can be live-worked anywhere. The F101, F102 and F104 power single segments with built-in isolation, power conditioning and termination.

Applications

  • FOUNDATION fieldbus DCS projects
  • Refineries, petrochemical plants and offshore platforms
  • Batch processes and solar-powered remote sites
