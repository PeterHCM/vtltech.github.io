Overview

Azbil (formerly Yamatake) controllers are known for stable PID control, widely used on furnaces, extruders and dryers. The range spans basic single-loop controllers to DIN-rail NX network modules linking to PLC and SCADA.

SR100/SR200 hybrid recorders log and display process data for quality control.

Applications

  • Temperature control of kilns, dryers and plastic machines
  • Process data recording and quality control

Viet Thang Loi Technology (VTL) is Azbil's partner in Vietnam. Contact VTL for model selection and a quotation.
